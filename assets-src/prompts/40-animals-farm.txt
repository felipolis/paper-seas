A sprite sheet of eight small animal cards in two rows of four, side views: a mallard duck floating (its body only, cut off straight across at the waterline, no water drawn), a mallard duck standing, a white hen, a yellow chick, a brown and white goat, a peach pig, a ginger cat sitting, a brown and white dog. Each animal is alone: no scenery, water, plants, ground or buildings inside, behind or under it, and the background shows between its legs. Every animal is built like the seagulls of the first reference: stacked cut-paper layers with pale lips, ending at a thin dark cut line, with no pale rim, halo or sticker border round it.
