A sprite sheet of three fern cards in one row. Every card is a low, wide fern clump, half again as wide as it is tall, on a flat base: many fronds of chunky leaflets fanning up and out from one point at the base, the lowest fronds lying out almost flat to both sides and arching down, the middle ones short. First fern: mixed sage green and pale green fronds with two olive brown fronds. Second fern: pale sage green fronds. Third fern: olive brown and golden brown fronds mixed with sage green fronds.
