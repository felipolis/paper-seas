A sprite sheet of seven animal cards in two rows (four, then three), side views: the standing ones stand on their own feet, with nothing under them: a red beach crab, a red and yellow parrot flying with wings spread, a brown monkey sitting, a coral-salmon flamingo standing on one leg, a pelican standing, a green iguana on a rock, a black and orange toucan flying. Every animal is built like the seagulls of the first reference: stacked cut-paper layers with pale lips, ending at a thin dark cut line, with no pale rim, halo or sticker border round it.
