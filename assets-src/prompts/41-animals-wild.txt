A sprite sheet of eight small animal cards in two rows of four, side views: a green frog sitting, a cream rabbit, a brown owl perched, a turquoise hummingbird, a tortoise, a brown wild boar, a little green gecko, a white heron standing. Every animal is built like the seagulls of the first reference: stacked cut-paper layers with pale lips, ending at a thin dark cut line, with no pale rim, halo or sticker border round it.
