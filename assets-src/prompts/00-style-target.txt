Tilt-shift macro photograph of a handmade pop-up book diorama lying on a table, seen from above at a 40 degree angle.

with a thin pale cut edge. In the middle a small three-masted pirate galleon made of folded and cut paper: dark brown printed
wooden hull with an ochre stripe and gun ports, cream cloth sails with stitched patches, a small black flag with a white skull,
thread rigging. Beside it a little tropical island made of layered paper: a sand-coloured card with a pale aqua shallow-water
ring, crossed die-cut palm tree cards printed with detailed illustrated fronds, bushes with tiny red flowers, grey rock cards and
a thatched hut card. Everything is cut-out cardstock with printed illustrations in a warm storybook style, crisp ink outlines,
visible paper fibre and tooth, soft contact shadows, warm golden lamp light from the upper left, shallow depth of field with the
ship in sharp focus and the edges softly blurred. Muted warm palette: teal, cream, ochre, terracotta, sage green, deep brown.
