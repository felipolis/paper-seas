A sprite sheet of nine ground cover cards in three rows of three. Every card is a long, low spreading drift like a strip of hedge border, two and a half times as wide as it is tall, with a gently rolling low top; the three cards of a row together span the sheet. Top row, three low spreading tropical bushes with small scallops along the top and small hibiscus flowers: a golden ochre bush with a lighter ochre inner layer and red flowers; an orange bush with an olive green inner layer and yellow and pink flowers; a teal bush with a lighter teal inner layer and yellow, red and pink flowers. Middle row, three wide drifts of pointed grass blades in mixed olive, yellow green and sage green. Bottom row, three flower drifts on medium stems: white daisies with yellow centres; small yellow flowers with leaves; a grass tuft drift with small orange flowers.
