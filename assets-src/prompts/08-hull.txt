One single card: the side elevation of a pirate galleon hull only, without masts or sails, filling the image width. The tall stern castle is on the left with small lit gallery windows and gold carving, the low bow is on the right with gold scrollwork, the hull is dark brown printed wooden planking with a wide ochre stripe holding a row of square gun ports with cannon muzzles, a dark red top strake and a gold rail with open balusters, and a strip of cream sea foam along the waterline at the bottom.
