A sprite sheet of three cloth sail cards in one row: three tall triangular lateen sails seen straight from the front, each a tall right triangle with a straight vertical left edge laced to a yard with a row of small lacing loops, a straight bottom edge and a long gently curved diagonal edge from the top left corner down to the bottom right corner. The first is warm cream canvas with printed seams, a broad crimson band along the bottom edge and a small gold sun emblem; the second is black weathered canvas, torn and frayed with holes, with faded bone white crossed bones; the third is pale grey green ghostly canvas, rotten and shredded into long tatters with big holes, with no printed pattern, only faded stains.
