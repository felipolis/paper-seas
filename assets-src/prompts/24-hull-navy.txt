One single card: the side elevation of a sleek royal navy frigate hull only, without masts or sails, filling the image width, long and low: the stern castle on the left with gilded gallery windows, the bow on the right with a carved beakhead and figurehead scroll, deep navy blue planking with a broad cream stripe holding a row of square gun ports with red lids and cannon muzzles, bright gold carving, a rail along the top with open balusters, and a strip of cream sea foam along the waterline at the bottom.
