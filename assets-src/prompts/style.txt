Style: flat frontal scans of handmade die-cut paper pop-up cards in exactly the craft of the reference images: the first shows finished sheets of this very set (match its craft, its layout on the key colour and its finish exactly, not its subjects); the second shows such cards standing in a world (copy only their craft: the cut shapes, the thin dark cut edges, the layered printed paper and its matte fibre; not its scene, camera or light).
Material: matte rigid cardstock; every piece is a printed layered paper collage of 2-4 stacked cut-paper layers, each upper layer with a thin pale lip along its upper-left edge and a short soft shadow on its lower right; 3-5 inks per piece in 3 or more value steps, a bold printed ornament in a contrasting accent (chevrons, zigzags, dots, stripes or scallops) only on plants, cloth, wood and buildings, while stone, water, sky and animal bodies keep plain printed shading with no pattern; chunky shapes with big countable motifs, nothing thin or spiky.
Edges: a thin dark warm-brown cut line round every silhouette, with a pale highlight just inside it on the upper-left edges only.
Palette: warm storybook colours: golden ochre, orange, terracotta, olive and sage green, teal, cream, warm brown.
Light: soft even light from the upper left.
Camera: flat orthographic front view, scanned flat, no perspective: each piece is one flat card lying on the scanner, never a folded or 3D paper model, with no fold or crease down its middle.
Layout: every piece separate and isolated, evenly spaced in the grid given, on one flat solid background of pure KEY, a colour that appears nowhere in the pieces (no KEY or near tints of it in any piece), with wide gaps; nothing touching, overlapping or cut off by the image edge; anything that stands has a flat straight base of its own, with no ground strip, patch, water or plinth under it; openings through a piece (an arch, between legs or branches) are cut out so the background shows through.
Avoid: felt, fabric, stitching, embroidery, plush or woven texture; an even white or cream border or rim round any piece (no sticker look); cast shadows on the background; grid lines, frames or dividers between pieces; glow; gloss or a CG render look; flat vector art; text, letters and numbers.
